The Room

The reception is held at Constellation, the hotel’s function space on the 27th floor, its top floor, with panoramic windows looking out over Manila Bay. Follow the signs from the lobby, lifts to Constellation are clearly marked, and hotel staff will be on hand to direct guests up.

About the Diamond Hotel

The Diamond opened in 1990, a 27 storey hotel set right on Manila Bay, its granite and art deco lines a Roxas Boulevard landmark for over three decades. It sits within easy reach of Rizal Park, Robinsons Place, and the Malate district, and last refreshed its rooms and public areas in 2017.

Guests travelling from Intramuros after the ceremony have a short trip along Roxas Boulevard to reach it, transport and timing for the day will be confirmed closer to April.
