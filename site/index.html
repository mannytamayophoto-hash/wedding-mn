Sa awa ng Maykapal at taglay ang basbas ng aming mga pamilya, pagbubuklurin po ang aming mga puso sa harap ng dambana ng Simbahan ng San Agustin, Intramuros, Maynila, sa pagsapit ng ikasampu ng Abril, taong dalawang libo dalawampu’t pito.

By the grace of the Creator and bearing the blessing of our families, our hearts will be joined as one before the altar of San Agustin Church, Intramuros, Manila, when the tenth of April, two thousand twenty seven, arrives.

The Invitation

Buong galak po namin kayong inaanyayahan sa aming kasal, sa ikasampu ng Abril, dalawang libo dalawampu’t pito, ikaapat ng hapon, sa Simbahan ng San Agustin, Intramuros, Maynila.

With full joy we invite you to our wedding, the tenth of April two thousand twenty seven, four in the afternoon, at San Agustin Church, Intramuros, Manila.

We are getting married on 10 April 2027 at the Iglesia de San Agustin in Intramuros, with the reception at the Diamond Hotel on Manila Bay. Everything you need is in this folio. Invitations and RSVP will follow. For now, save the date.
