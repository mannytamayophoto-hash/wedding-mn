A Word on Manila Traffic

Manila traffic is genuinely unpredictable, a trip that takes 10 minutes on a quiet morning can take an hour at the wrong time of day. Build in extra buffer for anything time sensitive, airport transfers, the ride to the ceremony, connections between hotels. Grab and taxis are easy to find, getting one quickly is rarely the problem, the road itself is.

Perth to Manila

Direct flights

  • Philippine Airlines PR224, 3 times weekly (Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday), approximately 7 hours, the only full service carrier flying direct. Departs Perth mid morning, around 8:35am, landing Manila mid afternoon, around 3:50pm.
  • Jetstar JQ80/JQ81, 3 times weekly (Wednesday, Friday, Sunday), the budget direct option. JQ81 departs Perth late evening, around 9:10pm, landing Manila 4:15am. JQ80 returns departing Manila early morning, around 5:35am, landing Perth 1pm.

Both routes run on the current 2026 schedule. Flight times and days this far ahead of April 2027 are not final, please reverify closer to booking.

One stop options

  • Singapore Airlines, via Singapore, several departures a day, earliest around 1:10am, others mid morning around 6:25 to 6:35am. Perth to Singapore is about 5 hours 30 minutes, Singapore to Manila about 3 hours 40 minutes, roughly 9 to 10 hours total depending on the layover.
  • Malaysia Airlines, via Kuala Lumpur, typically a very early departure from Perth, around 2:20am. Perth to Kuala Lumpur is about 6 hours, Kuala Lumpur to Manila about 4 hours, roughly 10 to 11 hours total depending on the layover.
  • Cathay Pacific, via Hong Kong, typically a morning departure from Perth, around 8:20am. Perth to Hong Kong is about 8 hours, Hong Kong to Manila about 2 hours 20 minutes, roughly 11 to 12 hours total depending on the layover.

Booking notes

Same time zone as Perth, so there is no jet lag either direction. Book 3 or more months ahead where you can, direct flights run only a few times a week and seats on the wedding weekend will move fast once other guests start booking.

Where to Stay

Diamond Hotel Philippines · Recommended

Roxas Boulevard, Manila Bay. This is the reception venue itself, Constellation on the 27th floor, so staying here means no transport to sort out after the reception. The bridal party will be staying at the Diamond for the whole weekend.

San Agustin Church is about 2 kilometres away, 10 to 15 minutes by car depending on traffic. A shuttle bus with limited seating will run guests from the Diamond to the church and back for the ceremony, register interest once the RSVP page is live. Taxis and Grab are also easy to find in the area if you would rather go under your own steam.

Best for: guests who want the simplest possible weekend, close to the ceremony, reception in the same building.

Inside Intramuros · Closest to the church

A small handful of hotels sit inside the walls themselves, as close to San Agustin as it gets.

  • The Bayleaf Intramuros, a boutique hotel inside the walled city, a short walk to the church, with a rooftop bar looking out over Manila Cathedral. Mid range.
  • White Knight Hotel, directly across from San Agustin Church itself, simply furnished and the most affordable option this close. Budget.

Best for: guests who want to roll out of bed and be at the church in minutes, no transport thinking required.

The Manila Hotel · For a historical stay

Rizal Park, at the edge of Intramuros. Open since 1912, one of the oldest hotels in the Philippines. San Agustin Church is about 1.1 kilometres away, roughly a 13 minute walk along Bonifacio Drive, or a 2 to 3 minute taxi ride. Given the April heat, a short taxi or tricycle is the more comfortable option on the day itself, the walk is a pleasant one to do another time, not in wedding dress.

Best for: history lovers, guests who want to be inside the old city rather than out on the bay.

Makati · For the CBD

Metro Manila’s financial and business district, about 6.5 kilometres from Intramuros, not walking distance, but a short taxi or Grab ride. Makati has the malls, the restaurants, and the nightlife if you want a base with more going on outside the wedding itself. Worth considering: The Peninsula Manila, Dusit Thani Manila, and New World Makati, ranging from five star luxury to mid range.

Best for: guests extending their trip, or who want easy access to shopping and dining beyond the wedding weekend.
